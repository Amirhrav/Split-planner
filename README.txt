Split Planner - iPhone home-screen app

1. Upload this whole folder to any HTTPS static host (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel).
2. On your iPhone, open the site's address in Safari.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
4. Open Split Planner from its new icon. It runs full screen and works offline after the first visit.

Your profile and workout log are stored on the phone inside the app. Use "Back up or restore your log" in the Log section to keep a copy.
To publish an update, replace index.html and change CACHE in sw.js (for example split-planner-v2).
